Pillar-Less Hall Architecture for Uninterrupted Views

Our venue features architectural design without middle columns or structural obstructions. This guarantees clear sightlines for every guest, making live stage performances, wedding rituals, and corporate presentations fully visible from every angle.

Key Benefits of Our Pillar-Less Layout

Unobstructed Stage View Every guest enjoys clear sightlines regardless of seating position.
Flexible Seating Configurations Easily adapt layouts for round-table dining, theater-style seating, or clusters.
Seamless Decor Setup Uninterrupted space allows mandap decorators and lighting designers complete creative freedom.
Live Event Ready Ideal for video recording, live streaming, photography, and multi-angle camera setups without visual blockages.

Spacious AC Venue Accommodating Up to 400+ Guests

RK Grande comfortably hosts up to 400+ guests (floating capacity) while maintaining space for dining, entertainment, photography setups, and stage decorations.

Comfortable Guest Circulation: Dedicated zones for ceremony seating and food service prevent crowding and maintain smooth guest movement throughout your function.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the maximum guest capacity at RK Grande?
RK Grande accommodates up to 400+ floating guests comfortably, making it suited for large weddings, corporate events, and social gatherings.
Does RK Grande feature a pillar-less hall design?
Yes, our banquet hall features 100% pillar-less architecture, providing unobstructed views of the stage and central hall from all seating areas.
Is parking available on-site for guests?
Yes, RK Grande provides spacious parking facilities along with professional valet assistance to manage guest vehicles smoothly.
Can the venue layout be customized for corporate events or themes?
Our flexible layout supports theater, cluster, round-table, and custom mandap/stage setups for traditional, contemporary, or corporate arrangements.
Does the hall provide power backup?
Yes, RK Grande has full power backup generators to ensure uninterrupted lighting, sound, and air conditioning throughout your event.
How do I book a site visit or check availability?
You can request a site visit by filling out our online booking form or calling our hospitality team directly via WhatsApp or phone.
