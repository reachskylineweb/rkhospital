Frequently Asked Questions

What hospitality services does RK Hospitality offer in Chennai?
RK Hospitality operates a comprehensive portfolio of hospitality brands including luxury banquet halls (RK Grande, RK Regal), multi-cuisine fine dining (GEM Royale), private igloo dining (Stardome), executive boardrooms (RK Nexus), and full-service event catering.
Are the venues at RK Hospitality suitable for weddings and large events?
Yes, RK Grande is specifically built for large-scale weddings, receptions, and corporate conferences accommodating up to 400+ guests with pillar-less visibility and dedicated dining space.
Does RK Hospitality provide executive meeting facilities for businesses?
Yes, RK Nexus offers fully equipped boardroom and conference facilities with high-speed internet, presentation displays, climate control, and catering support for corporate teams.
Where are RK Hospitality venues located?
All our venues are located in Kelambakkam along Rajiv Gandhi Salai (OMR), providing quick connectivity from Siruseri IT Park, Navalur, Padur, ECR, Thiruporur, and South Chennai.
Does RK Hospitality feature a family restaurant on OMR?
Yes, GEM Royale Restaurant is a multi-cuisine family restaurant offering delicious vegetarian, non-vegetarian, tandoori, and seafood specialties with valet parking.
